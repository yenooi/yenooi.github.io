Road to Guangdong (2020)

Purchase from Excalibur Games

Story-driving game available on Steam, PS4, XBox One, and Nintendo Switch now.

EMBARK ON A ROAD TRIP ADVENTURE

Road To Guangdong places you in the shoes of 23-year-old graduate Sunny, as she embarks on the road trip of a lifetime with her 68-year-old aunt Guu Ma, while attempting to maintain the weathered yet beloved family car, 'Sandy'. After Sunny unexpectedly inherits her family's restaurant, she sets out with Guu Ma to visit distant relatives, rekindle relationships, discover secret family recipes, and ultimately, reignite the family restaurant.

CHALLENGES ALONG THE WAY

Drive across the stunning Chinese province of Guangdong, as you attempt to keep the car alive throughout your journey. Dive under the hood, and tinker with car parts such as the engine, gearbox, oil filter, and so on. Keeping the old car alive won't be easy – manage petrol and oil levels to sustain the vehicle's integrity and reduce wear-and-tear. Build-up stock of useful items in your inventory, and avoid calling out the mechanic.

CHOICES AND OUTCOMES

In Road To Guangdong, navigate interesting narrative puzzles and moral choices. Your dialogue choices will shape Sunny's character, mould relationships with family members and determine various outcomes throughout the story. Join Sunny and Guu Ma on this road trip adventure that will test their relationship, and change both of their lives.

Thanks to Yen Ooi's strong writing, I was instead delighted by Guu Ma and the simple honesty of her character, one of the few in any video game I would say is a real, legitimate person...

Road to Guangdong will likely land within the top 10 games I've played in 2020 for its soulful narrative, peaceful gameplay, and surprising need for clever strategy. A small title with a massive personality, Road to Guangdong is on my list of must-have Switch games, and if you're even remotely interested in the game, it should be on yours as well. The distance between destinations is long and the travelling is slow-going, but the journey is insightful and certainly worth your time.
