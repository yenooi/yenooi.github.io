Thank you for dropping by. If this is your first time visiting, why not head to ‘Words’ and select ‘Highlights’. There, you can browse selected works in different formats.

I love working with words, especially bringing stories together with cultural and philosophical ideas. I believe that writing should always be simple in order to communicate clearly, and this, I think, is the most difficult task, ever.

My Substack is where I connect with readers, friends, and colleagues, so do join us if that is of interest to you. There, I offer a free subscription for occasional updates, and a paid subscription where I share short stories, poetry, articles, new project ideas, and more.

My other love is collaborating, and currently, I can also be found as a co-founder of Chapunk, co-Director of the London Science Fiction Research Community, co-editor of Ab Terra, co-guest editor of Vector-Zoefuturism, and violist for the London Video Games Orchestra.

If there’s something you can’t find here, do let me know.
